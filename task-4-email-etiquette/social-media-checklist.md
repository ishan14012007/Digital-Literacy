#The Social Media "Do" List

1.Do curate a professional profile (like LinkedIn) that highlights your skills, academic projects, and campus involvement.

2.Do audit your privacy settings regularly to ensure that personal posts are only visible to your intended audience.

3.Do use your platform to network with industry professionals, alumni, and peers in your field of study.

4.Do think before you post: ask yourself if you would be comfortable with a recruiter or a professor seeing the content.

5.Do set healthy boundaries for screen time to ensure social media doesn't interfere with your academic performance or sleep.

#The Social Media "Don't" List

1.Don't post content that depicts illegal activities, excessive partying, or anything that violates your university’s code of conduct.

2.Don't engage in "venting" or posting aggressive rants about professors, specific classes, or campus staff.

3.Don't share sensitive personal information, such as your real-time location, home address, or government ID numbers.

4.Don't participate in cyberbullying, exclusionary behavior, or toxic "call-out" culture that could damage your reputation.

5.Don't share unverified information or "fake news" that could undermine your credibility as a well-informed student.
